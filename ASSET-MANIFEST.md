# DallasRoofGuard asset manifest

## Visual direction

Trust-first local roofing photography for Dallas–Fort Worth homeowners. The set uses believable North Texas homes, charcoal architectural shingles, natural daylight, documentary framing, and restrained ember-orange safety accents. Images avoid fabricated branding, readable text, watermarks, unsafe work practices, and exaggerated storm damage.

## Generation mode

All photography was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then resized where needed and encoded as WebP at quality 84. Logo and map assets were created as project-native SVG graphics.

## Final photography prompts

### Hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: roofing company website hero. A newly completed premium architectural-shingle roof on an attractive North Texas brick home near Dallas, viewed from a slightly elevated three-quarter angle. Believable high-end documentary real-estate photography with natural materials and construction accuracy. Wide landscape, home and roof dominant, generous uncluttered sky and landscaping, suitable for both split hero crop and full-width overlay crop. Warm late-afternoon Texas sunlight, confident and welcoming, subtle ember warmth. Realistic roof geometry, charcoal dimensional shingles, mature neighborhood. No people, signs, company logos, readable text, watermark, or exaggerated HDR.

### Roof replacement

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: roof replacement service card and detail page. A pristine newly installed charcoal architectural-shingle roof on a refined North Texas suburban home, photographed from a safe low aerial perspective. Authentic professional construction photography with accurate flashing, ridges, vents, and shingle courses. Wide landscape with the roof filling most of the frame, strong diagonal rooflines, and enough context to read as a finished Dallas-area home. Clean bright morning light and a quiet sense of craftsmanship. No workers, vehicles, signs, logos, readable text, watermark, or distorted architecture.

### Roof repair

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: roof repair service card and detail page. Close-up of a professional roofer carefully repairing flashing and replacing charcoal asphalt shingles around a roof vent on a North Texas home. Candid documentary construction photography with realistic tools, gloves, safety equipment, and materials. Wide landscape detail with hands and repair area in sharp focus, worker only partially visible, shallow depth of field, and crop-safe composition. Neutral natural daylight; charcoal shingles, warm brick hints, and one restrained burnt-orange safety accent. Anatomically correct hands, technically plausible repair, and proper fall-protection gear. No logos, readable text, or watermark.

### Storm damage

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: storm and hail damage service card. Realistic hail and wind damage on an asphalt-shingle roof in North Texas, with a trained roofer inspecting bruised shingles and a few lifted tabs after a storm. Credible insurance-documentation photography, cinematic enough for marketing without becoming sensational. Wide landscape with the damaged roof surface and inspector's gloved hand clearly visible, and storm clouds receding in the background. Cool post-storm daylight with a subtle warm break in the clouds. Damage should be realistic and repairable; proper safety gear. No lightning, disaster spectacle, logos, readable text, or watermark.

### Roofing crew

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: roofing company about and craftsmanship section. Candid editorial construction photography, believable workers and equipment, authentic rather than staged. Wide landscape with two or three roofers distributed across the roof, a strong diagonal roof line, useful center crop, and home exterior and trees in the background. Warm early-morning light, calm teamwork, and pride in craft. Charcoal, warm brick, muted workwear, and restrained burnt-orange safety details. Correct fall protection and PPE, tidy site. No visible company logos, readable text, watermark, duplicated people, or impossible tools.
