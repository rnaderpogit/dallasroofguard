When it's time to replace

Signs a repair won't cut it

Curling shingles, granules collecting in the gutters, daylight in the attic, or a roof past 20 years are all signs you're due. We give you an honest assessment so you're never paying for a replacement you don't actually need.

  • Complete tear-off
    We strip down to the deck so problems can't hide under a second layer.
  • Deck inspection & repair
    Rotten or soft decking gets replaced before anything new goes down.
  • New underlayment & flashing
    Fresh moisture barrier and flashing at every valley, vent, and chimney.
What's included

Every replacement, the full package

No à la carte surprises. Each re-roof includes complete tear-off, deck inspection and repair, new underlayment and flashing, and full cleanup with a magnetic nail sweep — backed by both manufacturer and workmanship warranties.
